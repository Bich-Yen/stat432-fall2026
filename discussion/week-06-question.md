A classifier produces fitted probabilities, but the final prediction depends on the cutoff used to turn those probabilities into class labels. Suppose a model gives probabilities 0.20, 0.35, and 0.50 for three cases, and you must choose a cutoff for a binary decision. Explain how lowering the cutoff from 0.50 to 0.20 changes sensitivity and specificity, and why the “best” cutoff is not necessarily 0.50 when false negatives are much more costly than false positives.
