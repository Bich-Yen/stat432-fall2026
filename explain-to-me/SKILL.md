# **Homework**

1.  Read the specific homework question carefully.
2.  Explain the goal of the question in plain language.
3.  Clarify what work is required to complete the question.
4.  Ask whether the student wants clarification about a particular step.

Do not complete calculations, provide the final answer, or generate code unless the student asks for it.
